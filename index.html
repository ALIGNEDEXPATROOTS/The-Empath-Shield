Release Breath
~
Inhale through the nose for 4 counts. Hold for 2. Exhale through the mouth for 6 — push the last of the breath out. On each exhale, silently say 'Not mine.' Do this 6 times. Feel the borrowed emotions exit on every breath.
1 min
Body Shake
⌇
Stand if you can. Shake your hands out like you're flicking water off them. Let the shake travel up your arms, into your shoulders, your torso. Let your whole body shake it off for 60 seconds. Animals do this naturally after stress. So can you.
30 sec
Verbal Declaration
◉
Say aloud or in your mind: 'I release all energy, emotion, and thought that does not belong to me. It returns to its source, transformed into light. My field is clear. My field is mine.' Repeat three times with conviction.
3 min
Water Reset
≋
Wash your hands up to the elbows with intention. As the water runs, visualise grey or murky energy washing off your skin and down the drain. Cold water amplifies this. Dry your hands slowly and deliberately — this seals the reset.
5 min
Smoke Aura Sweep
∿
Light sage, palo santo, or incense. Start at the crown of your head and sweep slowly downward around your body. Move smoke around your back, under your arms, around your feet. Visualise the smoke lifting and neutralising anything that attached during your shift.

Longer-Term Protection Practices
Create a pre-shift ritual
Even 3 minutes of intentional shielding before you enter the building changes everything. The shield goes up when you decide, not when you're already overwhelmed.
Debrief your body, not just your mind
After shift, scan your body from crown to feet. Where is the tension? That's where you absorbed. Address it with breath, movement, or water before you take it home.
Recharge in your own energy
Silence in the car. A barefoot moment. Your own music. You need time between their energy and your home energy — every single day.
Name it to tame it
'This is not my grief, it is theirs.' Language creates separation. The moment you name it as transference, it loses its grip on you.

Energy Journal

Track your patterns over time. The more you document, the faster you'll recognise transference when it's happening — not hours later.
